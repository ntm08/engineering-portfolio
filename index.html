01 / ABOUT

Hardware meets intelligence.

I'm a Sydney-based engineering graduate with a Master of Mechatronics and Automation Engineering from Macquarie University and a Bachelor of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (Honours) from RMIT University Vietnam.

My work connects mechanical hardware, electronics and software. I enjoy taking a concept through prototyping, debugging and experimental validation — especially when cameras, sensors, microcontrollers and autonomous motion have to work together reliably.

I have worked in industry R&D on elevator-monitoring electronics and as a Sessional Teaching Academic at Macquarie University, supporting practical engineering projects in Wireless Mechatronics and Engineering Design.
